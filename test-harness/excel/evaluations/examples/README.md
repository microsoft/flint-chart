## About these charts

- Each chart is a native Excel chart object, not a canvas overlay or pasted image.
- Size, axes, date ticks, number formats and bar spacing come from Flint's layout; colours and fonts are Excel's own.
- Data, formatting, titles, axes, and series can be edited in Excel.

<details>
<summary><strong>How these examples were produced</strong></summary>

These images are unedited `Chart.getImage()` captures from the real Excel for Mac Office.js worker, at the size `assembleExcel` chooses. Regenerate them with:

```bash
node evaluations/real-gallery.mjs
node evaluations/export-workbook.mjs
node evaluations/update-examples.mjs
```

| Chart | Flint chart type | Native Excel chart | Data source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Palmer Penguins — flipper length vs body mass | Scatter Plot | `XYScatter` | Three species form crisp clusters — the modern replacement for the iris dataset (Palmer Station LTER, CC0). |
| Gapminder — life expectancy vs income per capita (2018) | Scatter Plot | `Bubble` | The Rosling bubble chart: wealth vs health, bubble size = population, colour = continent, on a log income axis (Gapminder / World Bank). |
| Most populous countries, 2023 (millions) | Bar Chart | `BarClustered` | The year India overtook China (UN World Population Prospects / World Bank). |
| Global temperature anomaly by decade (°C vs 1951–1980) | Bar Chart | `ColumnClustered` | Bars cross zero — cool early decades below, rapid warming above (NASA GISTEMP). |
| Electricity generation mix by country, 2023 | Stacked Bar Chart | `ColumnStacked` | How each country splits generation across sources (Our World in Data / Ember). |
| Titanic survival rate by class and sex | Grouped Bar Chart | `ColumnClustered` | "Women and children first" — and first class — are stark in the numbers (Encyclopedia Titanica). |
| Old Faithful — distribution of eruption durations | Histogram | `ColumnClustered` | Two humps: short (~2 min) and long (~4.5 min) eruptions — a mean would hide this (R "faithful" sample). |
| Average monthly temperature by city | Line Chart | `Line` | Warm bands for the tropics, cold winters for Moscow (climate normals). (drawn as an Excel-native chart; Flint draws it as a Heatmap) |
| Keeling Curve — atmospheric CO₂ at Mauna Loa | Line Chart | `Line` | The defining climate record: annual-mean CO₂ rising from 316 ppm (1959) to 421 ppm (2023) (Scripps / NOAA). |
| US unemployment rate, 2000–2023 (%) | Line Chart | `Line` | The 2009 financial-crisis plateau and the sharp 2020 pandemic spike (US Bureau of Labor Statistics). |
| Driving Shifts Into Reverse — miles vs gas price (US, 1956–2010) | Connected Scatter Plot | `XYScatterLines` | Miles driven per capita vs the price of gas, connected in year order — the classic self-crossing connected scatterplot (NYT / Hannah Fairfield; FHWA + EIA data). |
| Penguin body mass by species | Boxplot | `BoxWhisker` | Gentoo penguins are markedly heavier than Adélie and Chinstrap (Palmer Station LTER, CC0). |
| Desktop browser market share, 2024 | Pie Chart | `Pie` | Chrome dominates; Safari and Edge trail (StatCounter). |
| Mobile OS market share, 2024 | Donut Chart | `Doughnut` | Android vs iOS worldwide (StatCounter). |
| Life expectancy gap, male vs female (2021) | Grouped Bar Chart | `BarClustered` | The dumbbell length is the female–male gap in years (World Bank 2021). (drawn as an Excel-native chart; Flint draws it as a Ranged Dot Plot) |

| CO₂ emissions per capita, 2022 (tonnes) | Bar Chart | `ColumnClustered` | Per-person emissions vary ~20× across countries (Our World in Data / Global Carbon Project). (drawn as an Excel-native chart; Flint draws it as a Lollipop Chart) |
| Daily stock OHLC over two weeks | Candlestick Chart | `StockOHLC` | Open-high-low-close candles over ~10 trading days. |
